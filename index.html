Know Your Rights & GBV Legal Protections in Rwanda

Empower yourself with clear information on Law N°68/2018, survivor rights, emergency steps, and self-assessment tools.

Under Rwandan Law N°68/2018 of 30/08/2018 on prevention and punishment of Gender-Based Violence:

1. Right to Confidential & Free Medical Care
Every survivor of physical or sexual violence has the right to immediate, free emergency medical treatment, PEP (Post-Exposure Prophylaxis within 72 hours), and injury documentation.
2. Right to Privacy and Anonymity
The law strictly forbids exposing the identity of a GBV victim in media or public records without explicit consent.
3. Right to Legal Defense & Justice
GBV is a public crime in Rwanda. The Rwanda Investigation Bureau (RIB) and Prosecution handle cases free of charge.
4. Right to Psychosocial Counseling & Safe Shelter
Isange One Stop Centres provide free professional mental health therapy and temporary emergency protection.

Can I report if I am not sure I want to go to court?
Yes! Submitting an anonymous report on Vugana allows Isange staff to offer medical and psychological support without forcing legal charges if you are not ready.
Does Vugana cost any money to use?
No. Vugana is 100% free to access on any mobile phone or computer.
What happens if someone opens my browser while I am using Vugana?
Use the red 'Quick Exit' button at the top of the page. It will immediately redirect your screen to a neutral website.
